# Building V16: school classroom building

The school classroom building stands in Tucson, AZ. Plan north is true north.

It has 1 storey on a 210'-0" by 42'-8", with a wing of 52'-6" by 85'-4" extending from the west end of the north facade (see the drawing) footprint, 14'-0" floor to floor, with a flat roof and a slab on grade.
Model each floor as one thermal zone.

## Envelope

- Exterior walls: U-0.059 Btu/h-ft2-F (layers only, without surface films)
- Roof: U-0.042 Btu/h-ft2-F (layers only, without surface films)
- Windows: U-0.484 Btu/h-ft2-F (whole window), SHGC 0.28, visible transmittance 0.53
- Window-to-wall ratio by facade (as drawn): south 30 %, east 33 %, north 27 %, west 25 %; sill height 2'-11"
- Doors: one south facade door (as drawn), 3'-0" x 7'-0", insulated panel

## Internal loads and schedules

- Lighting: 0.76 W/sq ft
- Plug and process loads: 0.43 W/sq ft
- Occupant density: 20.31 people per 1,000 sq ft
- Hours of operation: weekdays 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., Saturdays closed, Sundays closed; no holidays.
- During operating hours occupancy is 95 %, lighting 90 % and plug loads 90 % of design; outside them occupancy is zero, lighting 5 % and plug loads 30 %.

## Air leakage

- Infiltration: 0.34 air changes per hour, constant.

## Systems and controls

- Each conditioned zone has a through-the-wall packaged terminal air conditioner with an electric resistance heating coil.
- Cooling efficiency: EER 11.6 (gross, rated)
- Thermostat: heating 70 F, cooling 75 F; outside operating hours setback to 60 F heating and setup to 85 F cooling, with recovery starting two hours before opening.

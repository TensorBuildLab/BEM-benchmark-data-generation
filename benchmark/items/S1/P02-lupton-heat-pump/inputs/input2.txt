Lupton Octagonal House (with a heat pump)

Overview. A single-story, single-zone ("Living Zone") traditional octagonal house in Lupton, AZ (35.06°N, 109.08°W, 1780 m elevation). 
It is electricity-only and has 4 occupants. 

Geometry
- The main footprint is a regular octagon with 19'4" (5.8928 m) sides, about 167.7 m² of floor.
- The north wall bulges out 8'2" (2.4892 m), adding about 14.7 m². The NW diagonal wall bulges out 7'8" (2.3368 m), adding about 13.8 m².
- Walls are 9" (0.2286 m) clay/adobe.
  - The rectangular wall section is 11'6" (3.5052 m) high.
  - Above it, each of the 8 faces has a segmental arch, 2'3" (0.6858 m) rise, also 9" clay. EnergyPlus models it as a flat rectangle.
  - A 10" (0.254 m) gap above the arch crown is modeled as a thin clay surface to close the envelope. The total wall envelope is 4.445 m (about 14'7") high.
- The roof is an arched wood roof, modeled as a flat horizontal surface at z = 4.445 m. It is a 1.5" wood deck plus 4" closed-cell spray foam, with U ≈ 0.2 W/m²K from the foam alone.
- The floor is a 4" concrete slab on grade.
- An internal mass of 14.86 m² of 9" wood side panels is included.

Internal gains and infiltration
- 4 people at 120 W each (activity schedule). They are home at night and on weekends, and mostly out from 08:00 to 17:00 on weekdays (10% present).
- LED lighting at 5 W/m², with a residential schedule.
- Appliances at 7 W/m², with a residential schedule.
- Infiltration is 0.000302 m³/s per m² of exterior surface, constant all year.

HVAC: a central, ducted, all-electric air-source heat pump
- It is one AirLoopHVAC with an AirLoopHVAC:UnitarySystem in blow-through order: supply fan, then DX cooling coil, then DX heating coil, then electric supplemental coil.
  - The fan is a continuous Fan:OnOff, 0.75 total efficiency, 250 Pa.
  - The cooling coil is single-speed DX with COP 3.666 (about SEER 13).
  - The heating coil is a reversible single-speed DX with COP 2.75. The compressor cuts out below −8 °C, and defrost is reverse-cycle, on demand.
  - The backup is an electric resistance coil with efficiency 1.0. It picks up the load below the compressor cutoff.
- The zone is served by a constant-volume, no-reheat terminal inside a ZoneHVAC:AirDistributionUnit.
- The thermostat is dual-setpoint: heating 21 °C (70 °F), cooling 24 °C (75 °F), all year, with no setback.
- Sizing is autosized (zone and system sizing both on). Outdoor air is 0.00944 m³/s per person, which is 4 people × 0.00944 ≈ 0.038 m³/s, and supply air temperatures are 12.8 °C for cooling and 50 °C for heating.

Fuel and use. All electric, with 4 occupants.

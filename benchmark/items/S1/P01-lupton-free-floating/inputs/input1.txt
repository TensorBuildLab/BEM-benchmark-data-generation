Lupton Octagonal House (passive, unconditioned)

Overview. A single-story, single-zone ("Living Zone") traditional octagonal house in Lupton, AZ (35.06°N, 109.08°W, 1780 m elevation). It has no HVAC at all: the indoor temperature floats freely with the weather. It is electricity-only and has 4 occupants. It is meant to be run with the nearest EPW, Gallup NM TMY3.

Geometry
- The main footprint is a regular octagon with 19'4" (5.8928 m) sides, about 167.7 m² of floor.
- The north wall bulges out 8'2" (2.4892 m), adding about 14.7 m². The NW diagonal wall bulges out 7'8" (2.3368 m), adding about 13.8 m².
- Walls are 9" (0.2286 m) clay/adobe.
  - The rectangular wall section is 11'6" (3.5052 m) high.
  - Above it, each of the 8 faces has a segmental arch, 2'3" (0.6858 m) rise, also 9" clay. EnergyPlus models it as a flat rectangle.
  - A 10" (0.254 m) gap above the arch crown is modeled as a thin clay surface to close the envelope. The total wall envelope is 4.445 m (about 14'7") high.
- The roof is an arched wood roof, modeled as a flat horizontal surface at z = 4.445 m. It is a 1.5" wood deck plus 4" closed-cell spray foam, with U ≈ 0.2 W/m²K from the foam alone.
- The floor is a 4" concrete slab on grade.
- An internal mass of 14.86 m² of 9" wood side panels is included.
